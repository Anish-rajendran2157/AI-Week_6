## Payroll and payslips

Salaries are paid monthly. Payslips are published in the payroll portal and are also
emailed as a password-protected PDF; the password is your employee ID in the format
EMP-##### followed by your date of birth as DDMM.

| Office | Pay date | Payroll cut-off for changes | Payslip available from |
|---|---|---|---|
| Chennai | last working day of the month | 18th of the month | 1 working day after pay date |
| London | 28th, or the prior working day | 15th of the month | on the pay date |
| Austin | 15th and the last working day | 8th and 22nd | on the pay date |

Corrections to pay are processed in the next cycle.

| Office | Employee cover | Family cover included | Employee monthly contribution | Enrolment window |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chennai | INR 500,000 sum insured | spouse, 2 children, parents | nil | within 30 days of joining |
| London | private medical scheme, band B | spouse and children, opt-in | GBP 22 | within 30 days of joining |

within 30 days of the event.

## Employee referral bonus

| Referred role grade | Bonus amount | Paid when |
|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | INR 25,000 / GBP 500 / USD 750 | after 90 days of referee service |
| L3 - L4 | INR 60,000 / GBP 1,200 / USD 1,800 | 50 percent at joining, 50 percent at 180 days |
| L5 and above | INR 125,000 / GBP 2,500 / USD 3,500 | 50 percent at joining, 50 percent at 180 days |
| Niche or hard-to-fill (flagged by recruiting) | 1.5x the band amount | as per band |

The referrer must be a confirmed employee and must not be the hiring manager. The bonus
is taxable and is paid through payroll.

## Travel and expense reimbursement

Business travel needs pre-approval from your reporting manager, and from the function
head for any international trip.

| Expense item | Limit or rule |
|---|---|
| Hotel per night, within India | INR 6,500 |
| Hotel per night, London | GBP 190 |
| Hotel per night, within US | USD 210 |
| Meals per day (India / UK / US) | INR 1,800 / GBP 40 / USD 65 |
| Mileage (India / UK / US) | INR 12 per km / GBP 0.45 per mile / USD 0.67 per mile |
| Claim submission window | 45 days from the expense date |

Economy class applies to flights under 6 hours. Alcohol, fines and family travel are not
reimbursable. Relocation costs are dealt with in the Relocation Policy.

## Performance review in brief

The review year runs 1 April to 31 March. Ratings are released in April and increments
take effect on 1 June.

| Rating | Label | Increment multiplier |
|---|---|---|
| 5 | Outstanding | 1.8x |
| 4 | Exceeds expectations | 1.3x |
| 3 | Meets expectations | 1.0x |
| 2 | Partially meets | 0.4x |
| 1 | Does not meet | nil |

A rating of 1, or two consecutive ratings of 2, leads to a performance improvement plan
of 60 days. An employee who disagrees with a rating may ask the skip-level manager to
review it within 10 working days of release.

## Conduct and grievances in brief

Employees are expected to act with honesty, protect customer data, declare conflicts of
interest, and not accept gifts worth more than a nominal amount. Breaches are handled
under the disciplinary procedure: investigation, hearing, written outcome, with a right of
appeal within 7 working days.

Raise a concern first with your reporting manager, then your People Ops partner, then the
function head, and finally the Ethics Committee. Grievances are acknowledged within 3
working days and concluded within 20 working days.
