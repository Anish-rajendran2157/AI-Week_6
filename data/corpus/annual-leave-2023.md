## 1. Purpose

Northwind Systems provides paid annual leave (also called earned leave in India) so that
employees can rest, travel and attend to personal matters. This policy is retained for
reference for leave years up to and including FY2024-25.

## 2. Scope

Applies to all permanent employees across Chennai, London and Austin. Interns,
contractors and agency staff are covered by separate commercial terms and are not
eligible. Employees serving a notice period may take leave only with written approval
from their reporting manager.

## 3. Entitlement

| Grade band | Annual leave days | Max carry-over into next year | Accrual rate per month |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L3 (Associate to Senior Engineer) | 18 | 5 | 1.50 |
| L4 - L5 (Lead, Principal) | 21 | 7 | 1.75 |
| L6+ (Director and above) | 24 | 7 | 2.00 |
| Fixed-term (under 12 months) | 10 | 0 | 0.83 |

Leave accrues monthly and is credited on the last working day of each month. New joiners
accrue from their date of joining, pro-rated to the nearest half day.

## 4. Carry-over and encashment

Unused leave up to the carry-over cap in the table above may be carried into the next
leave year. Carried leave must be consumed by 30 June or it lapses. Encashment of leave
is permitted only at the time of separation and is capped at 10 days regardless of grade
band.

## 5. Applying for leave

1. Raise the request in the HR portal at least 7 working days in advance (21 working days
   for any absence of more than 5 consecutive days).
2. Your reporting manager approves or rejects within 5 working days.
3. For absences over 7 consecutive days, the skip-level manager must also approve.
4. Update your calendar and hand over active work items before the first day of leave.
5. Leave taken without an approved request is treated as loss of pay.

## 6. Public holidays

Public holidays are published per office in January and are not deducted from annual
leave. Employees who work on a declared public holiday may claim compensatory off within
30 days.
