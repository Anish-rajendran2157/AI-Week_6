## 1. Scope and employment at will

Applies to employees on the US payroll based at the Austin office or working remotely
within the United States. Employment is at will: either the employee or the company may
end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without
notice. Nothing in this policy creates a contract of employment or a guaranteed notice
period.

## 2. Introductory review period

| Grade band | Introductory review period | Extension allowed | Requested notice during the period |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 90 days | up to 60 days, once | 1 week |
| L3 - L4 | 90 days | up to 60 days, once | 2 weeks |
| L5 and above | 90 days | not permitted | 2 weeks |

The introductory review period is used for structured feedback and does not change the
at-will nature of employment. Benefits eligibility begins on the first of the month
following the date of hire and is not tied to this period.

## 3. Requested notice on resignation

| Grade band | Requested notice | Treated as eligible for rehire if given | Unused PTO paid out |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 2 weeks | yes | per state rule, see table note |
| L3 - L4 | 3 weeks | yes | per state rule, see table note |
| L5 and above | 4 weeks | yes | per state rule, see table note |

Table note: accrued unused paid time off is paid out at separation only where the
employee's work state requires it. Texas does not require payout, so Austin-based
employees do not receive a payout unless a written agreement provides otherwise.

## 4. Resignation procedure

1. Notify your reporting manager in writing of your intended last working day.
2. Submit the separation request in Workday within 1 working day of that notification.
3. The People Ops partner schedules the offboarding checklist and confirms the final pay
   date and benefits end date.
4. Return all company property to the IT asset desk on or before the last working day.
5. Complete the voluntary exit survey.

## 5. Benefits at separation

Health coverage ends on the last day of the month in which employment ends. Continuation
coverage information is mailed by the benefits administrator within 14 days of the
separation date. The 401(k) administrator contacts the employee separately regarding the
account.

## 6. Involuntary separation

Where the company ends employment, final pay is provided within 6 calendar days of the
separation date. Severance, where offered, is discretionary and set out in a separate
written agreement.
