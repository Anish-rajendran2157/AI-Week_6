## 1. Intent

Northwind Systems expects employees who are unwell to stay away from the workplace and
recover. Sick leave is a separate bucket from annual leave and cannot be encashed or
carried over.

## 2. Entitlement and certification

| Office | Paid sick days per leave year | Self-certified days (no document) | Medical certificate required from | Pay during extended medical leave |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Chennai (IN) | 12 | 2 consecutive | day 3 | 60 percent of basic for days 13-90 |
| London (UK) | 10 | 7 consecutive | day 8 | statutory sick pay from day 11 |
| Austin (US) | 8 | 3 consecutive | day 4 | short-term disability from day 9 |

Sick leave does not accrue monthly; the full entitlement is credited on 1 April or on the
date of joining, whichever is later, and is pro-rated for joiners after 1 October.

## 3. Notification procedure

1. Inform your reporting manager before the start of your working day, by phone or chat.
   Email alone is not sufficient notification.
2. Record the absence in Workday on the same day, or on the first day back if you were
   unable to.
3. Upload a medical certificate where the table above requires one. Certificates are
   stored by the People Ops partner and are not visible to your reporting manager.
4. For any absence of more than 5 consecutive working days, the People Ops partner will
   open a wellbeing check-in before you return.
5. A return-to-work conversation with your reporting manager is mandatory after any
   absence of 10 or more consecutive working days.

## 4. Extended medical leave

Where an illness or injury requires absence beyond the paid sick days above, the employee
may apply for extended medical leave. Approval is given by the People Ops partner in
consultation with the business unit head. Extended medical leave is capped at 90 calendar
days in any rolling 12-month period.

## 5. Misuse

Patterns of single-day absences adjoining weekends or public holidays may be reviewed.
Where sick leave is found to have been claimed dishonestly, the matter is handled under
the Code of Conduct and may result in recovery of pay.

## 6. Interaction with other leave

Sick leave taken during an approved annual leave period is not converted back to annual
leave. Sickness during parental leave is governed by the regional Parental Leave Policy.
