## 1. Principle

Northwind Systems operates a hybrid model. Teams are expected to be co-located for
collaborative work and to use remote days for focused individual work. This policy
replaces the 2023 Remote Working Policy.

## 2. Office attendance expectation

| Role family | Minimum office days per week | Anchor days set by | Fully remote permitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering and QA | 3 | team lead | by exception only |
| Product and Design | 3 | product head | no |
| Sales and Customer Success | 2 | regional sales head | yes, for field roles |
| Support (24x7 rota) | 4 | support operations manager | no |
| Corporate functions (Finance, People, Legal) | 3 | function head | no |

Anchor days are the specific weekdays on which the whole team attends. Anchor days are
published by the team lead each quarter and cannot be changed with less than 2 weeks
notice.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement requires approval by the function head and the People Ops
partner, is reviewed every 6 months, and may be withdrawn with 60 days notice. Fully
remote employees must attend the office for at least 5 days per quarter, and travel for
those days is reimbursed under the Travel and Expense Policy.

## 4. Home office support

| Item | Amount | Frequency | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time home office setup | INR 25,000 / GBP 350 / USD 450 | once per employment | after confirmation |
| Internet allowance | INR 1,500 / GBP 25 / USD 35 | per month | all employees |
| Monitor or chair refresh | 60 percent of cost, capped | once every 3 years | fully remote only |

## 5. Procedure for ad hoc work from home

1. Inform your reporting manager by the end of the previous working day.
2. Mark the day in the team calendar as WFH.
3. Remain reachable during your team's core hours.
4. Ad hoc work from home beyond 6 days in a calendar month requires written approval from
   the function head.

## 6. Working from another country

Working from a country other than your payroll country is permitted for a maximum of 20
working days in any rolling 12 months, subject to prior approval by the People Ops
partner and the tax team. Longer arrangements are treated as a transfer and fall under
the Relocation Policy.

## 7. Core hours

Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 local time. Meetings spanning Chennai, London and Austin
should be scheduled in the 14:00 to 17:00 UK window where possible.
