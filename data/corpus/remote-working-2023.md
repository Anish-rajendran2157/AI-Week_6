## 1. Principle

Northwind Systems supports remote working where the role allows it. Teams are expected to
be co-located for collaborative work and to use remote days for focused individual work.
This policy applies to the period up to 31 March 2025.

## 2. Office attendance expectation

| Role family | Minimum office days per week | Anchor days set by | Fully remote permitted |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engineering and QA | 2 | team lead | yes, with function head approval |
| Product and Design | 2 | product head | yes, with function head approval |
| Sales and Customer Success | 1 | regional sales head | yes |
| Support (24x7 rota) | 3 | support operations manager | yes |
| Corporate functions (Finance, People, Legal) | 2 | function head | yes |

Anchor days are the specific weekdays on which the whole team attends. Anchor days are
published by the team lead each half year and cannot be changed with less than 1 week
notice.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement requires approval by the function head, is reviewed every 12
months, and may be withdrawn with 30 days notice. Fully remote employees must attend the
office for at least 2 days per quarter.

## 4. Home office support

| Item | Amount | Frequency | Eligibility |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time home office setup | INR 15,000 / GBP 200 / USD 250 | once per employment | after confirmation |
| Internet allowance | INR 1,000 / GBP 15 / USD 20 | per month | fully remote only |
| Monitor or chair refresh | 50 percent of cost, capped | once every 4 years | fully remote only |

## 5. Procedure for ad hoc work from home

1. Inform your reporting manager by the start of the working day.
2. Mark the day in the team calendar as WFH.
3. Remain reachable during your team's core hours.
4. Ad hoc work from home beyond 10 days in a calendar month requires written approval
   from the function head.

## 6. Working from another country

Working from a country other than your payroll country is permitted for a maximum of 30
working days in any rolling 12 months, subject to prior approval by the HR business
partner.

## 7. Core hours

Core hours are 12:00 to 16:00 local time.
