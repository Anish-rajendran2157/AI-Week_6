## 1. Scope

Applies to employees on the UK payroll based at the London office. Contractual notice in
the individual contract of employment prevails where it is longer than the table below.

## 2. Probation

| Grade band | Probation duration | Extension allowed | Notice during probation (either side) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 3 months | up to 3 months, once | 1 week |
| L3 - L4 | 3 months | up to 3 months, once | 2 weeks |
| L5 and above | 3 months | not permitted | 4 weeks |

A probation review meeting is held at the midpoint and again in the final two weeks. The
outcome is recorded in writing by the reporting manager and countersigned by the People
Ops partner. Successful completion is confirmed by letter.

## 3. Notice period after probation

| Grade band | Employee resignation | Termination by company | Payment in lieu of notice |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 1 month | 1 month | at company discretion |
| L3 - L4 | 2 months | 2 months | at company discretion |
| L5 and above | 3 months | 3 months | at company discretion |

Statutory minimum notice from the company increases with service and is applied where it
exceeds the contractual figure. Notice runs in calendar months from the date the
resignation is received in writing.

## 4. Garden leave and restrictions

The company may place a departing employee on garden leave for all or part of the notice
period. During garden leave the employee remains employed, continues to receive full pay
and benefits, and must remain available but must not contact clients or attend the
office. Post-termination restrictions in the contract of employment continue to apply.

## 5. Resignation procedure

1. Give written notice to your reporting manager, stating the intended last working day.
2. The reporting manager forwards the notice to the People Ops partner within 2 working
   days.
3. The People Ops partner issues an acknowledgement letter and confirms the last working
   day and any accrued leave balance.
4. Complete the handover plan and return all company property.
5. Attend an exit conversation; attendance is optional but encouraged.

## 6. Final pay and references

Final pay is made in the payroll run for the month of leaving, or the next run where the
last working day falls after the payroll cut-off. Accrued untaken annual leave is paid
out; leave taken in excess of accrual is recovered. The company provides factual
references confirming job title and dates of employment only.
