## 1. Scope

Applies to employees on the US payroll based at the Austin office or working remotely
within the United States. Northwind Systems provides company-paid parental leave in
addition to any unpaid job-protected leave available under federal or state law.

## 2. Entitlement

| Leave type | Company-paid weeks | Service required | Must be used within | Pay level |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Birth parent (primary carer) | 12 | 90 days | 12 months of birth | 100 percent of base |
| Non-birth parent (secondary carer) | 6 | 90 days | 12 months of birth | 100 percent of base |
| Adoption or foster placement (primary) | 12 | 90 days | 12 months of placement | 100 percent of base |
| Adoption or foster placement (secondary) | 6 | 90 days | 12 months of placement | 100 percent of base |
| Pregnancy loss | 2 | none | not applicable | 100 percent of base |

Company-paid parental leave runs concurrently with unpaid job-protected leave where both
apply. It may be taken in a single block or in up to three blocks of at least one full
week each.

## 3. Medical and disability interaction

Birth parents may be eligible for short-term disability benefits for the recovery period.
Short-term disability runs before company-paid parental leave begins and does not reduce
the 12 weeks in the table above. The benefits administrator confirms eligibility.

## 4. Benefits continuity

Health, dental and vision coverage continue unchanged during parental leave and the
employee contribution continues to be deducted from payroll. Where there is no pay in a
period, the employee is invoiced for the contribution and must settle it within 30 days.

## 5. Procedure

1. Notify your reporting manager and the People Ops partner at least 30 days before the
   intended leave start date, or as soon as practicable.
2. Submit the parental leave request in Workday with the employee ID format EMP-#####.
3. Provide documentation of birth, placement or expected date when available.
4. The People Ops partner confirms the approved blocks and the return date in writing.
5. Contact the benefits administrator separately for any short-term disability claim; the
   People Ops partner does not file this on your behalf.

## 6. Return to work

There is no minimum return-to-work period and no repayment condition. Nursing parents are
provided a private room at the Austin office and reasonable break time for the first 12
months after birth.
