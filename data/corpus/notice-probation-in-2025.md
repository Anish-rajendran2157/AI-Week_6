## 1. Scope

Applies to employees on the India payroll. Notice periods and probation durations differ
by office; a request that does not state the office cannot be answered from this file
alone.

## 2. Probation and confirmation

| Grade band | Probation duration | Extension allowed | Notice during probation (either side) |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 6 months | up to 3 months, once | 30 days |
| L3 - L4 | 6 months | up to 3 months, once | 30 days |
| L5 and above | 3 months | not permitted | 30 days |

Confirmation is not automatic. The reporting manager must submit a confirmation
recommendation in the 5th month of probation. Where no recommendation is submitted,
probation is treated as extended by one month and the People Ops partner escalates to the
business unit head.

## 3. Notice period after confirmation

| Grade band | Employee resignation | Termination by company | Maximum buy-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| L1 - L2 | 60 days | 60 days | 30 days |
| L3 - L4 | 90 days | 60 days | 45 days |
| L5 and above | 90 days | 90 days | not permitted |

Notice is counted in calendar days from the date the resignation is acknowledged in
writing, not from the date the email was sent. Annual leave may not be used to offset
notice except with the written approval of the business unit head.

## 4. Resignation procedure

1. Submit the resignation in Workday and send a copy to your reporting manager.
2. The reporting manager acknowledges within 2 working days and proposes a last working
   day.
3. The People Ops partner issues the acceptance letter confirming the last working day
   and any buy-out amount.
4. Complete the handover checklist and the knowledge transfer document.
5. Return laptop, access card and any hardware to the IT asset desk, and complete the
   no-dues clearance across IT, Finance and Facilities.
6. Attend the exit interview with the People Ops partner in the final week.

## 5. Full and final settlement

Full and final settlement is paid within 45 days of the last working day. Leave
encashment, gratuity where applicable and recovery of any buy-out or notice shortfall are
netted in that settlement. The relieving letter and experience letter are issued only
after clearance is complete.

## 6. Absconding

An employee absent without approval for 7 consecutive working days and unreachable is
treated as having abandoned employment, and the matter proceeds under the Disciplinary
Policy.
