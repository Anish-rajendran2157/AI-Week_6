## About this page

This FAQ is maintained by the People Ops team as a quick reference. Where it differs from
a policy document, the policy document is authoritative. It was last reviewed shortly
after the FY2025-26 policy refresh and some answers still reflect earlier practice.

## Leave

**How many leave days do I get?**
Most employees get 20 days of annual leave a year. Leads and above get more. The exact
number for your grade band is in the Annual Leave Policy table.

**How much leave can I carry forward?**
You can carry forward up to 10 days. Anything above that lapses at the end of the leave
year. Carried days must be used by the end of the second quarter.

**Do I need to apply in advance?**
Yes. One week of notice is the general expectation for short absences, and a month for
anything longer than a week.

**Can I cash out my leave?**
Only when you leave the company, and only up to 15 days.

**Is sick leave part of my annual leave?**
No, it is a separate balance. Chennai employees have 12 paid sick days; London and Austin
have fewer. You do not need a certificate for a single day off.

## Attendance and working location

**How many days do I need to be in the office?**
Two to three days a week depending on your team. Your team lead publishes anchor days.

**Can I work from home for a whole week?**
Ad hoc work from home is fine with your reporting manager's agreement. If it becomes more
than a few days in a month you need the function head to sign off in writing.

**Can I work from abroad while visiting family?**
Short periods are possible with approval from the People Ops partner. The limit is
roughly a month a year and there are tax considerations.

**What are core hours?**
11:00 to 16:00 in your local time zone.

## Expenses and other common questions

**How long do I have to submit an expense claim?**
Submit within a month of incurring the expense. Older claims need a written justification
to Finance.

**Who do I ask about something not covered here?**
Raise a ticket with People Ops, or speak to your People Ops partner. For anything about
moving offices between countries, ask about the Relocation Policy.
