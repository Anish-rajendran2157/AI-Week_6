## 1. Scope

Applies to employees on the India payroll based at the Chennai office, including those
working remotely within India. Employees on the UK or US payroll are covered by the
policy for their own region.

## 2. Entitlement

| Leave type | Paid duration | Eligibility service at expected date | Notice to employer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Maternity leave (first two children) | 26 weeks | 80 days worked in preceding 12 months | 10 weeks |
| Maternity leave (third child onward) | 12 weeks | 80 days worked in preceding 12 months | 10 weeks |
| Paternity leave | 5 working days | 6 months | 6 weeks |
| Adoption leave (primary carer, child under 3 months) | 12 weeks | 12 months | 6 weeks |
| Surrogacy (commissioning parent) | not covered | not applicable | not applicable |
| Pregnancy loss or termination | 6 weeks | none | not applicable |

Maternity leave may begin up to 8 weeks before the expected date of delivery. Paternity
leave must be taken within 3 months of the date of birth and must be taken as a single
block.

## 3. Work from home on return

Employees returning from maternity leave may work from home full time for up to 2 months
after their return date, by agreement with the reporting manager.

## 4. Creche and nursing

The Chennai office provides a nursing room on the third floor. A creche reimbursement of
INR 5,000 per month per child is available until the child turns 4, claimed through the
monthly expense process.

## 5. Procedure

1. Notify your reporting manager and the HR business partner in writing, giving the
   expected date and the planned leave start date.
2. Submit Form MAT-IN with your employee ID in the format EMP-##### and supporting
   medical documentation at least 10 weeks before the leave start date.
3. HR confirms the entitlement and the return date in writing within 15 working days.
4. Agree a handover plan with your reporting manager before the leave start date.
5. Confirm your return date at least 4 weeks before returning, or request an extension
   as loss of pay.

## 6. Related

See the Annual Leave Policy for the treatment of leave accrued during maternity leave.
