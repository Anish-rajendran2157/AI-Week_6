## 1. Scope

Applies to employees on the UK payroll based at the London office. The entitlements below
are enhanced above statutory minimums and are not transferable to the Chennai or Austin
offices.

## 2. Entitlement

| Leave type | Full pay | Then half pay | Then statutory or unpaid | Total available |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Maternity leave | 18 weeks | 8 weeks | 26 weeks | 52 weeks |
| Adoption leave (primary carer) | 18 weeks | 8 weeks | 26 weeks | 52 weeks |
| Paternity or secondary carer leave | 6 weeks | none | none | 6 weeks |
| Shared parental leave | 12 weeks | 8 weeks | balance of 52 weeks | 50 weeks |
| Unpaid parental leave (per child, to age 18) | none | none | 18 weeks | 18 weeks |
| Neonatal care leave | 12 weeks | none | none | 12 weeks |

Enhanced maternity and adoption pay is conditional on 26 weeks of continuous service at
the 15th week before the expected week of childbirth. Employees with less service receive
the statutory entitlement only.

## 3. Keeping in touch days

An employee on maternity, adoption or shared parental leave may work up to 10 keeping in
touch days without ending the leave. These are paid at the normal daily rate and must be
agreed with the reporting manager in advance. Keeping in touch days are voluntary on both
sides.

## 4. Repayment condition

Where an employee does not return to work for at least 13 weeks after enhanced maternity
or adoption leave, the enhanced element above statutory pay is recoverable on a pro-rata
basis. The statutory element is never recoverable.

## 5. Procedure

1. Notify your reporting manager and the People Ops partner no later than the 15th week
   before the expected week of childbirth.
2. Provide the MATB1 certificate or the adoption matching certificate.
3. The People Ops partner issues a written confirmation of the leave dates, the pay
   pattern and the expected return date within 28 days.
4. Complete a risk assessment with the office facilities lead where applicable.
5. Give at least 8 weeks notice if you wish to change your return date.

## 6. Annual leave during parental leave

Annual leave continues to accrue throughout parental leave at the rate in the Annual
Leave Policy, and the carry-over cap does not apply to leave accrued while on parental
leave.
